# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-07)

First release: 16 modules in two tracks, every number computed by the C engine in the browser.

**Using AI:** How it works, Why answers vary, Long tasks, Context, Tokenization,
Calibration, What you want to hear, Retrieval.

**Under the hood:** Shrinking a model, Fitting in memory, Embeddings, Sampling settings,
LoRA, Learning rate, Overfitting, Evaluating a model.

Also in this release:

- Brazilian Portuguese: an EN | PT switch in the sidebar translates every module. The
  text the toy models read stays in English, so the numbers match in both languages.
- Every claim about real models is checked against a source, listed at the end of each
  module and at the top of its copy file.
- The engine now works out the averages, totals and worked examples that the pages
  used to compute in JavaScript.
- A page for missing addresses, link previews for sharing, and an MIT licence.
